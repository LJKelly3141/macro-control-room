Dashboard harness

Query: ?rounds=N steps N rounds on load; ?theme=light|dark forces a theme; ?one=1 plays one universe (Keynesian, boom, full width); ?scoring=off turns scoring off (no green band). Round 2 choice: spending +3, rate −1. Gauges and AD/AS get the no-policy record for the same round (null in round 0); ?np=0 turns it off. After round 1 the optimal benchmark is computed per universe (PU.optimal.benchmark) and its record for the same round is passed as opts.optimal; ?opt=0 turns it off. One universe plays 12 rounds with choices at rounds 2 (rate +1), 3 (spending −2), 5 (rate +0.5), 7 (spending +1).
